The width truth

Lanes overlap or the bed stays striped; the 0.8 overlap norm (labeled) is the honest tilling width. Bed width against honest inches is the lane count - three honest lanes, a working rhythm, or a lawn of lanes.

The depth truth

The claimed depth is the third pass through loose soil. The honest first pass reaches 0.6 of the claim, the second 0.85 (labeled norms) - your target against the two numbers: one pass and done, two passes deep, or a third pass, honestly.

The bed truth

Passes times square feet at the 0.5 square-feet-a-minute norm (labeled) is the honest clock on the bed - done before the sprinkler cycle, a working afternoon, or a two-weekend bed. The brochure's soil tills itself; yours has roots.

What it never does

It never counts the overlap as new ground, never calls the third pass the first, never schedules the bed out of the weekend, and never forgets the norms are labeled - the last lane at dusk keeps the honest ledger.

Tiller Math is illustrative arithmetic with labeled norms, not a measurement of your tiller, your soil or your bed. Follow the manual's safety instructions.
